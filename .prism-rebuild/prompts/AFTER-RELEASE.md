# Continue PRISM after the overhaul

Begin from the released source/artifact identity and current roadmap. Review observed issues, support reports and measurements. Prioritize correctness, recoverability and repeated-work reduction before speculative infrastructure. Preserve compatibility and migrate schemas deliberately.

For each approved enhancement, define outcome, scope, budget implications, dependencies and acceptance cases; implement a representative slice, review it, expand coverage and release through the established gates. Use focused revisions and component-scoped approvals rather than restarting whole designs.

Refresh knowledge only when a source, standard or measured project behavior materially changes. Retain provenance and superseded guidance. Add useful learning as a concise conditional rule with a check; do not accumulate duplicate prompts or raw source material. Human research may be activated as a separate explicitly scoped activity; its previously deferred status is not retroactive evidence.
